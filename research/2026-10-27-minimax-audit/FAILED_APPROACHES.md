# Failed approaches and limits

1. **Unchanged arbitrary-law Psi conjecture: not resolved.** Exact two/three-scale checks found no counterexample. Finite grids do not establish unrestricted minimax behavior. The new finite arbitrary-law upper bracket has no proved universal comparison with Psi. A finite decision-tree minimax optimization and justified arbitrary-law approximation theorem were not completed.
2. **Critical work using infinite inverse-square moments fails.** The inherited alpha>2 proof integrates D^-2, which diverges at alpha=2. Capping the classifiers and assigning uniform weights to their finite scale families repairs finiteness but leaves H=1+log(1+log(1/rho)). The failing matching lemma is elimination of that H from uniform mean work without losing O(rho) second moment. No necessity theorem for H is known here.
3. **One-law stopping transfer fails for fresh reservoirs.** Query fresh coins until a random walk first reaches +1. Priors 5/8 and 3/8 at D=1/2 give mean work 4 under the first but nontermination 2/5 under the second; an interior-gap variant is also proved. This does not refute the fixed-finite theorem or the minimax lower bound's uniform budget. Concurrent Tier5 already restricts its stopping conclusion to the appropriate law.
4. **Density smoothness does not imply cross-state smoothness.** The explicit observed regression example in the note estimates at B^-1/2 despite a linear margin. It excludes a generic transfer of the squared-log lower bound to structured simulators. Conditional-normal and joint-density comparison assumptions also cannot silently contain the Bernoulli hard subclass.
5. **Unknown rho exact saturation remains impossible at interior fixed gap.** The inherited stopped-law absolute-continuity argument is valid. Our majority/deconvolution construction instead has an explicit additive floor and logarithmic loss; neither is proved necessary. The deterministic endpoint is an exception and is treated separately.

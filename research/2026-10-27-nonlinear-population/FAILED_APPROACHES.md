# Failed routes and limits

* **Pilot-fit survivor calibration:** a fitted target or shallow likelihood fit does not control `integral(q-qhat)(g-1)dmu`. Explicit moment-matched profiles have different targets and identical capped-depth observations even under feedback. No uniform-q upper bound was obtained. This does not exclude a shallow pilot followed by sufficiently deep production.
* **Remove feedback without loss:** false in general under the exact deterministic two-flip comparison. The full-Q certificate gives a strict minimax separation despite optimal nonlinear terminal estimation in the comparison class. It does not show a logarithmically growing gain.
* **Amplify the small two-flip information gain into the critical rate:** the checked equal-prior expansion gives order rho^4 improvement against leading rho^2 risk. No valid amplification producing the missing factor was found.
* **Infer clipped risk from raw variance:** false without a tail premise; independent rare seed noise increases raw variance arbitrarily with an arbitrarily small clipping penalty. A uniform kurtosis or clipping-displacement condition restores a restricted transfer, but neither is automatic for randomized telescopes.
* **Sum the depth-tail hard pairs across K:** invalid because the pair changes with K. The theorem is an unrestricted necessary depth condition, not an integrated logarithmic lower bound.
* **Treat a finite prior's posterior as uniformly calibrated:** invalid. The implemented heuristic uses a design prior and faces an out-of-prior test profile. Favorable finite point estimates do not settle misspecification, arbitrary laws or joint rare-event limits. The candidate is not accepted as a uniform or stable practical improvement.
* **Treat a finite computation as the expected-budget optimum:** invalid without approximation errors. The separate theorem now supplies explicit truncation, rounding, parameter-grid and budget-restoration errors. No general finite convex program was solved, and its size may be enormous.

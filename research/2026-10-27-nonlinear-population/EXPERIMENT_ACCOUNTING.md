# Frozen experiment accounting clarification

The frozen contract's zero pilot/calibration/training expense means zero additional **statistical observations** and no empirical tuning stage. It does not mean zero deterministic computation: prior construction uses a known-law80-step bisection for one profile and prediction calculations. The completed run's total wall time includes that setup. Per-estimate timing excludes Model construction and shares a cache of deterministic predictive probabilities across methods, budgets and replicates. These row timings are not one-shot operational cost estimates and cannot support a speedup or break-even claim. This clarification does not change the frozen method, seeds, models, budgets, outputs or evaluation criteria.

No algorithm sees the actual test q, magnitude or sign. The simulator has those values to generate bits; estimate receives only a bound flip callback and the Model built from known mu,rho. The finite prior is a design choice, not a true-q oracle. A posterior based on it need not be calibrated under a profile outside its support.

All four acquisition policies spend exactly the stated number of genuine flips. The constant-zero, half and rarity-midpoint comparators spend zero flips. Posterior outputs already lie in[0,rho]; raw and [0,1]-clipped values are both retained. No estimator-supplied confidence procedure exists, so no coverage number is reported.

The analysis reports bounded-loss Hoeffding uncertainty under the ideal independent-stream simulation model. The actual implementation uses Python's seeded pseudorandom generator and float64 arithmetic; this is numerical evidence, not certified arithmetic or a rigorous probability guarantee for the pseudorandom outputs. Cellwise intervals are not simultaneous across the suite.32replicates yield wide intervals and prohibit a stable-superiority interpretation. The finite rational feedback separation is a separate mathematical result and does not borrow support from these noisy numbers.

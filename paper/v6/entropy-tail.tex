\section{The entropy--tail law for every magnitude law}\label{sec:entropy-tail}
The variational modulus $\Psi$ of \cref{sec:rare} is a valid lower
bound, but \cref{thm:v5-critical} shows that it cannot be matched in
general. This section states the replacement. The experiment is that of
\cref{sec:rare-model}, with every budget $B>0$ allowed. The upper
procedure may know $\mu,\rho,B$; magnitudes and signs remain hidden. No
density, regular variation, positive lower support bound, finite inverse
moment, or finite full-law entropy is assumed.

\subsection{Proof structure}
The upper construction in \cref{sec:entropy-tail-upper} races a
likelihood bank against an ordinary capped classifier on independent
observation streams of the same latent unit. Its null likelihood uses
the full law $\mu$. A mixed-work bound and finite Poisson multilevel
allocation give the three-piece upper bound.

The lower proof in \cref{sec:matching-lower} begins with an optimal
finite-band selection. Its ordinary and unresolved pieces are controlled
by a variational null-work modulus. Optimality constrains the gaps of
the retained bands. Grouping comparable costs and trimming a controlled
number of the smallest gaps produces annular components with admissible
conditional rarities and active mass-saturation constraints. Allocating
rarity proportionally to retained entropy makes their lower bounds add
at every budget. Finite-prefix approximation covers laws accumulating
at zero.

All specialized ingredients are proved in
\cref{sec:uf-foundations,sec:lower-foundations,sec:transfer-foundations}:
the ordinary classifier, weighted likelihood bank, homogeneous work sum,
multilevel allocation, weighted sign entropy, scale-sensitive gate,
null-work transfer, and additive localization. Standard concentration,
minimax, and convex-duality tools are cited where used.
\Cref{sec:related-work} distinguishes these ingredients and their
antecedents from the contribution of the joint selection law.

\subsection{Fractional-submeasure confidence localization}
\label{sec:additive-confidence}

The common-pair theorem combines across overlapping fractional
submeasures of the known gap law.

\begin{theorem}[Verified additive confidence localization]
\label{thm:additive-confidence-verified}
Let $\sigma_i$, $i\in I$, be finitely many nonzero submeasures with
$\sum_i\sigma_i\le\mu$, and write
$\tau_i=\sigma_i((0,1/2])$ and $\nu_i=\sigma_i/\tau_i$.
Choose $0<r_i\le1/4$ with $\sum_i\tau_i r_i\le\rho$.
For each conditional law $\nu_i$, suppose every capped bounded
observable one-coin estimator with uniform bias at most $b_i>0$
and uniform variance at most $V$ on $X_{r_i}(\nu_i)$ satisfies
$V\mathbb E_{0,i}T\ge J_i>0$.
Then, for $B>0$ and $0<\delta\le1/4$,
\[
 r_{\rm exp}(B,\delta)\ge
 \frac1{128}\sum_i\tau_i
 \min\left\{b_i,\sqrt{\frac{J_i\log(1/\delta)}{B}}\right\}.
\]
This holds for the original hidden-gap observation model, with a
uniform expected budget over the global rarity class.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Put $\ell=\log(1/\delta)$ and
\[
 s_i=\frac1{32}\min\{b_i,\sqrt{J_i\ell/B}\},
 \qquad \kappa=\frac{42\ell}{1024B}.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:common-pair-verified} supplies component pairs
$u_i,v_i$ with target differences at least $s_i$, both priors
bounded by $1/2$, and local Hellinger bounds
\[
 1-\Aff(P_{u_i}^A,P_{v_i}^A)
 \le \frac{42s_i^2}{J_i}\mathbb E_{0,i}T_A
 \le \kappa\mathbb E_{0,i}T_A.
\]
Each pair works for every capped local acquisition rule and
independent auxiliary seed.

Let $\eta=\mu-\sum_i\sigma_i$ and form the positive-sign measures
\[
 \zeta_q=\sum_i\tau_i u_i\nu_i,\qquad
 \zeta_r=\sum_i\tau_i v_i\nu_i.
\]
They satisfy $2\zeta_q,2\zeta_r\le\mu$. Define measurable global
priors by clipping their Radon--Nikodym densities at $1/2$.
The clipping preserves the densities almost everywhere, so
$q\mu=\zeta_q$ and $r\mu=\zeta_r$ exactly as measures.
Consequently
\[
 \theta(r)-\theta(q)\ge\sum_i\tau_i s_i,\qquad
 \theta(q),\theta(r)\le\sum_i\tau_i r_i\le\rho.
\]
The negative-sign measure is
$\eta+\sum_i\tau_i(1-u_i)\nu_i$, with the analogous identity
for $r$. These identities hold when the component supports overlap.
The
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/b64f44f43aa0bb2c43f83ad428a4bc9627a5f773/lean/ThresholdHC/AdditiveConfidence/PositiveMass.lean}{global prior construction}
checks the exact density and target identities.

\clearpage
For any fixed capped local procedure, push these measures through
the Bernoulli-parameter map, the independent coin-tape generator,
and the actual stopped observation map. Its finite sufficient
statistic has laws
\[
 P=P_{\rm res}+\sum_i\tau_i P_i,\qquad
 Q=P_{\rm res}+\sum_i\tau_i Q_i,
\]
where the shared residual has total mass $\eta((0,1/2])$.
The finite Cauchy--Schwarz inequality gives
\[
 \Aff(P,Q)\ge
 \eta((0,1/2])+\sum_i\tau_i\Aff(P_i,Q_i).
\]
The full stopped transcript has the same affinity as this statistic.
Since the residual mass plus $\sum_i\tau_i$ equals one,
\[
 1-\Aff(P_q^A,P_r^A)
 \le\sum_i\tau_i\bigl(1-\Aff(P_{u_i}^A,P_{v_i}^A)\bigr).
\]
The all-negative work law decomposes with the same weights and a
nonnegative residual contribution. Therefore
\[
 \sum_i\tau_i\mathbb E_{0,i}T_A\le\mathbb E_0T_A,
 \qquad
 1-\Aff(P_q^A,P_r^A)\le\kappa\mathbb E_0T_A.
\]
Thus the combined pair satisfies the local premise of the original
stopped $16/3$ KL transfer. The
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/b64f44f43aa0bb2c43f83ad428a4bc9627a5f773/lean/ThresholdHC/AdditiveConfidence/LocalBound.lean}{formal mixture bound}
includes both the actual observation laws and their work comparison.

If an estimator had confidence radius smaller than the theorem's
right-hand side, quarter smoothing and midpoint testing would give
\[
 \frac{\ell}{4}\le\frac{16}{3}\kappa B
 =\frac{7\ell}{32},
\]
which contradicts $\ell>0$. The bound holds for every admissible
observable estimator and hence for their minimax infimum.
Both conclusions for the normalized submeasures are
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/b64f44f43aa0bb2c43f83ad428a4bc9627a5f773/lean/ThresholdHC/AdditiveConfidence/Full.lean}{checked in Lean}.
\end{proof}

The Lean construction normalizes each nonzero submeasure, retains
the exact residual, and proves both the parameter-law identity and
the work decomposition before invoking the stopped information bound.
The entropy-packing application requires its conditional
variance--null-work obstructions in addition to this theorem.

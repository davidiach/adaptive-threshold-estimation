\subsection{The sharper general-law confidence coefficient}
\label{sec:entropy-confidence}

A direct comparison between retained entropy and the stopping
program gives the paper's stronger confidence coefficient.

\begin{theorem}[Verified explicit confidence law]
\label{thm:entropy-confidence-explicit}
For every known positive-gap law $\mu$, $0<\rho\le1/4$,
$B>0$ and $0<\delta\le1/4$, write $\beta=B/\log(1/\delta)$.
Then
\[
 10^{-17}Q(\beta)\le r_{\rm exp}(B,\delta)
 \le r_{\rm hard}(B,\delta)\le750000\,\mathcal K(\beta).
\]
In particular, the same lower coefficient holds for
$\mathcal K(\beta)\le Q(\beta)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First work at an arbitrary budget $b>0$. Put
$P=P_\infty(b)$, which is finite. For the retained prefix entropy
$F$, write $M=\min\{\rho,\sqrt{\rho F/b}\}$.
In the packing case, every attained finite-cap certificate $C$
satisfies the already verified bounds
\[
 M\le256\left(256b_C+
          \sqrt{16384e^{48}V_C}\right),\qquad
 256b_C+\sqrt{16384e^{48}V_C}
 \le6\cdot10^{12}\sqrt{b_C^2+V_C}.
\]
Here $b_C$ and $V_C$ are the certificate's bias and variance prices.
The certificate is attained at every positive integer cap $N$,
and $\sqrt{b_C^2+V_C}=P_N(b)$.
Since $256\cdot6\cdot10^{12}\le16\cdot10^{14}$, taking the
infimum over $N\ge1$ gives
\[
 M\le16\cdot10^{14}P.
\]
The
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/0feee44f1e6dbdee6e3d8edb6c11eec2bc39ed60/lean/ThresholdHC/ConfidenceEntropy/Core.lean}{formal retained-core bound}
holds at the exact cap-program infimum.

For completeness, consider the other branches of the deterministic
quantile comparison. Let $t$ be the closed $\rho$-quantile,
$h=b^{-1/2}$, and $T$ the clipped inverse-gap tail modulus.
The observable quantile-tail lower bound and $R(b)\le P$ give
$T\le32P$. If $t\le h$, the same lower bound gives
$Q(b)\le32P$ directly.

Otherwise choose the first band cutoff below $t$.
When $F\le262144$, the label lower bound gives
$M\le8192P$. If the packing cutoff fails, its threshold
inequality gives
$M\le2097152T\le67108864P$.
Both are smaller than $16\cdot10^{14}P$.
Together with the packing case, the deterministic comparison yields
\[
 Q(b)\le T+2M
 \le(32+32\cdot10^{14})P.
\]
The exact numerical inequality
\[
 30\cdot10^{-17}(32+32\cdot10^{14})<1
\]
therefore proves $30\cdot10^{-17}Q(b)\le P_\infty(b)$.
The
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/0feee44f1e6dbdee6e3d8edb6c11eec2bc39ed60/lean/ThresholdHC/ConfidenceEntropy/Program.lean}{formal comparison}
covers all cutoff cases.

Apply this at $b=\beta$ and use the checked stopping-program
confidence bound
$r_{\rm exp}(B,\delta)\ge P_\infty(\beta)/30$.
The comparison $\mathcal K\le Q$, the inclusion of hard-cap
procedures in the expected-budget class, and the existing
hard-cap upper bound complete the result. The
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/0feee44f1e6dbdee6e3d8edb6c11eec2bc39ed60/lean/ThresholdHC/ConfidenceEntropy/Full.lean}{full theorem}
also gives the lower bound for each admissible observable estimator.
\end{proof}

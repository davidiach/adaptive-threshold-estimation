\section{A uniform convergence rate for the optimized cap programs}
\label{app:uniform-cap-rate}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:uniform-cap-rate}
Let $\mu$ be any gap law, $0<\rho\le1/2$, $B>0$, and let $N\ge1$ be an
integer. For $k>1$ the programs of \cref{sec:genie} satisfy
\[
 N\ge\frac{B}{(1-1/k)^2(k^2-1)}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 G_N(B)\le P_\infty(B)\le P_N(B)\le kG_N(B).
\]
In particular $N=\lceil4B/3\rceil$ suffices for $P_N\le2G_N$ and
$R(B)\le P_N(B)\le16R(B)$. For $N\ge B$ the factor may be taken to be
$1+2(B/N)^{1/3}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix an admissible quartet, let $m=(q_L+q_H)/2$ and
$\Delta=q_H-q_L$. For $0<e<1$ mix both references with $m$:
$v'=(1-e)q_v+em$ and $w'=(1-e)q_w+em$. Convexity preserves global
rarity. Write $K$ for the original ordinary information/work ratio at cap
$N$. An infinite $K$ gives zero in the original dual, so suppose it is
finite.

Fix a root-active policy and let $W_u$ denote its expected paid work
under prior $u$. Before depth $N$, the variance reference at every word
is at least $(1-e)$ times the old one. Consequently the sum of ordinary
stop rewards before the cap is bounded by $K W_{q_w}/(1-e)$, since
including ordinary stops at the cap can only increase that original
information. This uses the closed perspective also on null words.

At a gap $d$, $|\Delta|\le2m$ and $|\Delta|\le2(1-m)$. The positive and
negative latent contributions to the reveal reward
\eqref{eq:genie-terminal} therefore satisfy
\[
 \frac{\Delta^2}{v'}\le\frac{4m}{e},\qquad
 \frac{\Delta^2}{1-v'}\le\frac{4(1-m)}{e}.
\]
A zero denominator has zero contrast. Multiply by the respective word
likelihoods, integrate in $d$ and sum over terminal stops. The reveal
information is at most $4\Pr_m(T=N)/e\le 4W_m/(eN)$.
An independent policy seed may be conditioned on throughout.
Affineness of work gives $W_{w'}=(1-e)W_{q_w}+eW_m$, so division and
maximization over policies give
\[
 K_N^+(q_L,q_H;v',w')
 \le \max\{K/(1-e)^2,\,4/(e^2 N)\}.
\]
Set $e=1-1/k$. The cap condition implies
$4+B K_N^+(q_L,q_H;v',w')\le k^2(4+BK)$.
The target separation did not change. Maximizing the original quotient
in \eqref{eq:exact-four-prior-dual} gives $P_N\le kG_N$ by
\eqref{eq:genie-program}. The other inequalities follow from
\cref{thm:genie-program}. For $k=2$ the cap condition is $N\ge4B/3$.
For $t=(B/N)^{1/3}\le1$ and $k=1+2t$, its denominator is
$16t^3(1+t)/(1+2t)^2\ge t^3$.
\end{proof}

The bound concerns the optimized programs. An individual unregularized
quartet can have infinite genie information at every cap, as in
\cref{sec:genie}. The optimal uniform convergence rate remains open.

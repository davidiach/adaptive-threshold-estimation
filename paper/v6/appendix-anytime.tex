\section{Observable time-uniform confidence for a known gap law}
\label{app:anytime}

Fix a known positive-gap law $\mu$, $0<\rho\le1/4$ and
$0<\delta\le1/4$. The \href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/ffc0c5eb436ff31706d6f46fc135d1831b5a4e61/lean/ThresholdHC/Anytime/Full.lean}{time-uniform theorem} gives a single online procedure with nested intervals
$I(B)$ such that, uniformly over all rare sign priors,
\[
 \Pr\{\theta\in I(B)\text{ for every }B\ge0\}\ge1-\delta.
\]
It purchases at most $\lfloor B\rfloor$ flips by the output at budget $B$.
On the simultaneous-coverage event its half-width, for every $B\ge1$,
is at most
\[
 1500002\,\mathcal K\!\left(\frac{B}{\Lambda(B,\delta)}\right),\qquad
 \Lambda(B,\delta)=\log(1/\delta)+
 2\log\!\left(2+\frac{\log(1+B)}{\log2}\right).
\]
The same coverage event protects the interval at any data-dependent stopping
budget. The theorem proves an upper width bound. The general law-dependent
time-uniform lower bound and adaptation to an unknown gap law remain open.

\paragraph{A prescribed sequence of observable stages.}
Stage $j$ reserves $s_j=4\cdot2^j$ flips and receives failure allocation
$\delta_j=\delta/[(j+1)(j+2)]$. The total allocation is at most $\delta$.
The fixed-confidence upper theorem of Appendix~\ref{app:known-confidence}
and a strict margin above its infimum supply a stage rule with cap $s_j$
and radius
\[
 r_j=750001\,\mathcal K\!\left(\frac{s_j}{\log(1/\delta_j)}\right).
\]
The selection functional is positive and finite at these positive budgets,
so the extra unit in the coefficient gives an attaining rule at this radius.
Choose the whole sequence before observing any data, with separate private
seeds and coin-label namespaces.

The controller starts with $[0,\rho]$. On completion of stage $j$, it clips
the stage estimate to $[0,\rho]$ and intersects the current interval with
the ball of radius $r_j$ around that estimate. It accepts a decision after
the stage's last reserved flip. It spends whole blocks, discarding filler
observations after an early internal stop. Thus stage $j$ finishes after
$p_{j+1}=4(2^{j+1}-1)$ purchased flips. For any budget, output the most recent
completed intersection.

\paragraph{One experiment for all budgets.}
Both endpoints are measurable functions of the same private seed and stopped
observation history. Increasing the budget preserves every earlier action
and \href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/ec31ac9931c8464fe89f5a4b043a6b2a23e4a65b/lean/ThresholdHC/Anytime/Compatibility.lean}{observed prefix}. The simulation identity maps the original hidden-gap
population onto the independent stage inputs in this shared experiment.
A pathwise-capped stage has stopped by its final reservation almost surely,
and clipping can only decrease its absolute error. Therefore its completed
center has failure probability at most $\delta_j$. A union bound over all
stages gives simultaneous coverage. Intersecting retains the true parameter
on that event, and choosing a stopping budget after seeing the observations
retains the same guarantee.

\paragraph{Width between stage completions.}
Let $k$ be the number of completed stages at budget $B$. Then
$p_k\le B<p_{k+1}$ and
$k\le\log(1+B)/\log2$. If $k=j+1>0$, these inequalities imply
$B<4s_j$ and $\log(1/\delta_j)\le\Lambda(B,\delta)$.
Monotonicity and the square-root budget scaling of $\mathcal K$ give
\[
 r_j\le750001\,\mathcal K\!\left(\frac{B}{4\Lambda(B,\delta)}\right)
       \le1500002\,\mathcal K\!\left(\frac{B}{\Lambda(B,\delta)}\right).
\]
The running intersection is contained in the last stage's ball. If $k=0$,
then $B<4$, and the selection functional at $B/\Lambda$ equals $\rho$.
The initial interval therefore satisfies the same bound.

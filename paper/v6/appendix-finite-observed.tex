\section{The finite-cell observed-state lower bound}
\label{app:finite-observed}

This appendix formalizes \cref{prop:outer} for arbitrary measurable
selected-state policies, including revisits, interleaving, private
randomness, free outer samples, and unbounded stopping.

\begin{theorem}[Verified finite-cell obstruction]
\label{thm:finite-observed-verified}
Let $\mu$ be any probability law on $(0,1/2]$, let $0<h\le1/2$,
and let $0<\delta\le1/8$. There is a finite measurable partition
$(A_i)$ of $[0,1]$, with cell magnitudes $d_i\in(0,1/2]$ and
weights $w_i=|A_i|$, such that
\[
 \sum_{i:d_i\le x}w_i\le F_\mu(x),\qquad
 \Delta=\frac14\sum_i w_i\min\{1,h/d_i\}
 \ge\frac18\omega_\mu(h)>0.
\]
For each deterministic sign array $s$, take
$X\in\{-1,1\}$ with conditional mean
$(2s_i-1)2d_i$ on $A_i$. Its target is
$\theta_s=\Pr(\mathbb E[X\mid U]>0)=\sum_iw_i s_i$.
Every selected-state estimator satisfying
\[
 \sup_s\Pr_s(|\widehat\theta-\theta_s|>\Delta/4)\le\delta,
 \qquad \sup_s\mathbb E_sN\le B
\]
obeys
\[
 B\ge\frac{225}{128h^2}
       \operatorname{kl}(1-2\delta,2\delta).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Raise each gap to at least $h$, then apply the finite upward rounding
grid with distortion two. This gives a measurable finite-range map $T$
with $d\le T(d)$ and
\[
 \min\{1,h/d\}\le2\min\{1,h/T(d)\}.
\]
Write its distinct values as $b_j$ and their probabilities as $a_j$.
Then $\Delta=\frac14\omega_{T_\#\mu}(h)\ge\omega_\mu(h)/8>0$.
Choose an integer $M>0$ with
$M^{-1}\le\delta\Delta^2/16$.
A measurable image of uniform measure on $[0,1]$ realizes
$\mu\otimes\operatorname{Unif}\{1,\ldots,M\}$ exactly.
The fibers of the pair consisting of the rounded-gap label and the
replication index form the cells. They have weights $a_j/M$ and
magnitudes $b_j$. Upward rounding gives the CDF inequality, and
\[
 \sum_iw_i^2=\frac1M\sum_j a_j^2
 \le\frac1M\le\frac{\delta\Delta^2}{16}.
\]
Place independent signs on these cells with probabilities
$q_i^\pm=1/2\pm\eta_i$, where
$\eta_i=\frac18\min\{1,h/d_i\}$.
Their target means differ by $\Delta$.
Under either product prior, the target variance is at most
$\sum_iw_i^2/4$. Consequently,
\[
 \Pr_\pm\bigl(|\theta_s-\mathbb E_\pm\theta_s|>\Delta/8\bigr)
 \le\frac{16\sum_iw_i^2}{\Delta^2}\le\delta.
\]
Thresholding the estimator at the midpoint of the two target means
therefore gives a test with each error at most $2\delta$.

Each query consumes one observation from its cell's independent
Bernoulli tape. The Bayes mixture realizes the adaptive simulator's
finite-prefix laws, and its expected physical work is at most $B$.
Since $0\le\eta_i\le1/8$ and $d_i\eta_i\le h/8$, the checked
stopped-transcript bound gives
$\operatorname{KL}(\mathbb P_+,\mathbb P_-)\le(128/225)h^2B$.
Binary data processing supplies
$\operatorname{kl}(1-2\delta,2\delta)$ as a lower bound.
Combining the inequalities proves the result.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Adaptive selection of component types}
\label{sec:typed-confidence}

The confidence transfer also holds when the algorithm chooses the type
of each fresh coin using its private seed and previous observations.
Each type has a known gap law and a fixed pair of conditional sign
priors. A coin retains its type, gap and sign on every revisit.

\begin{theorem}[Verified selected-type information transfer]
\label{thm:typed-confidence-verified}
Let the component types form a finite nonempty set. For type $i$, let
$\mu_i$ be a probability law on $(0,1/2]$ and let $q_i,r_i$ be measurable
conditional sign probabilities bounded by $1/2$. Suppose that for every
capped observable single-coin procedure, with any independent auxiliary
randomness,
\[
 1-\Aff(P_{i,q_i},P_{i,r_i})\le K\,\mathbb E_{i,0}T,
 \qquad K\ge0.
\]
The observation law here retains the auxiliary seed and the actual
stopped coin prefix. Form the smoothed priors
\[
 s_i=\frac{3q_i+r_i}{4},
 \qquad t_i=\frac{q_i+3r_i}{4}.
\]
For every measurable adaptive population policy with independent
private randomness, if $\mathbb E_s T\le B$ and
$\mathbb E_tT<\infty$, then its complete observable laws satisfy
\[
 \KL(\mathbb P_s\Vert\mathbb P_t)\le\frac{16}{3}KB.
\]
The conclusion allows countably many persistent coin labels,
interleaved revisits, adaptive type choices and unbounded stopping.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Give type $i$ an infinite supply of labels $(j,i)$, $j\in\mathbb N$.
For $k$ types, encode $(j,i)$ by $jk+i$. This is a bijection, and the
decoded type of that label is always $i$. The formal
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/1f6a773aa1176a35cd83930d4d37b7251439c03e/lean/ThresholdHC/TypedConfidence/Labels.lean}{label identity}
equates the physical observation words before and after this encoding.
An arbitrary policy on these labels therefore includes adaptive type
selection and persistent revisits.

First fix a deterministic policy, horizon $n$ and finite label family.
Let $\kappa_j,\tau_j$ be the two unsmoothed Bernoulli parameter laws at
label $j$. Every consistent word has probability
\[
 a(w)\prod_j M_j(w;\lambda_j),
 \qquad
 M_j(w;\lambda_j)=\int L_j(w;p)\,d\lambda_j(p),
\]
where $a(w)$ is the policy-consistency indicator and $L_j$ is the
likelihood of the observations of label $j$. These probabilities are
the exact marginals of independent persistent tapes. Quarter
smoothing makes the two local supports equal. The logarithm of the
product ratio gives the
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/1f6a773aa1176a35cd83930d4d37b7251439c03e/lean/ThresholdHC/TypedConfidence/ParameterHybrid.lean}{hybrid identity}
\[
 \KL(P_s\Vert P_t)=\sum_j\KL(P_s\Vert P_{s,j\to t}).
\]
In the $j$th term, simulate all other tapes under the same reference
law $s$ and retain them as independent auxiliary randomness.
Data processing from the genuine local transcript and the $8/3$
quarter-smoothing bound control that term by $8/3$ times its
unsmoothed Hellinger loss.

Because $s_j\le1/2$, all-negative expected work is at most twice
the reference expected work. Each local simulation therefore costs
at most its reference contribution times $(16/3)K$.
The local contributions sum exactly to the full expected number of
physical observations. This proves the finite bound.

\clearpage
At each finite horizon, a deterministic binary decision tree can use
only finitely many labels. Restriction to those labels preserves its
exact word law. Moreover, a word visits at most $n$ distinct labels,
so the likelihood ratio of the smoothed word laws is at most $3^n$,
independently of the largest label. This bound permits integration
over an arbitrary measurable private seed while keeping the seed
in the observable transcript. The
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/1f6a773aa1176a35cd83930d4d37b7251439c03e/lean/ThresholdHC/TypedConfidence/SeededTransfer.lean}{randomized prefix bound}
is consequently
\[
 \KL(P_{s,n}\Vert P_{t,n})\le
 \frac{16}{3}K\,\mathbb E_s T_n.
\]

The simulated path is valid at every step. Its stopped symbols agree
with the policy's stopping decisions. Finite expected work at each
endpoint implies almost-sure stopping. On each finite stopping
event, padding the stopped prefix by stopped symbols reconstructs the
complete observable seed and tape. The
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/1f6a773aa1176a35cd83930d4d37b7251439c03e/lean/ThresholdHC/TypedConfidence/StoppedTransfer.lean}{entropy reconstruction identity}
therefore yields
\[
 \KL(\mathbb P_s\Vert\mathbb P_t)
 =\sup_n\KL(P_{s,n}\Vert P_{t,n})
 \le\frac{16}{3}KB.
\]
The
\href{https://github.com/davidiach/adaptive-threshold-estimation/blob/1f6a773aa1176a35cd83930d4d37b7251439c03e/lean/ThresholdHC/TypedConfidence/SignTransfer.lean}{final sign-law theorem}
expresses this bound using the actual known gap laws and conditional
sign priors.
\end{proof}

The local policy-uniform Hellinger premise remains an explicit
hypothesis of this transfer. Section~\ref{sec:common-pair-confidence}
constructs that pair from a variance--null-work obstruction.
Fractional-submeasure localization retains its paper proof.

\section{A direct proof of the closed quantile law for finite laws}\label{app:finite-direct}
This appendix proves Theorem~\ref{fg:thm:direct-main}. Notation follows
\cref{fg:sec:model}: signs are $S\in\{-1,+1\}$, $h=B^{-1/2}$ is the
budget scale, and $\mathfrak h$ is squared Hellinger distance. The proof
uses the single-unit and transfer results of
\cref{sec:uf-foundations,sec:lower-foundations,sec:transfer-foundations},
the entropy-tier lemma of \cref{sec:matching-lower}, and the upper
construction of \cref{sec:entropy-tail-upper}. It does not use
Theorem~\ref{thm:entropy-tail-law}.

\begin{abstract}
We estimate the fraction $\theta=\Prob(P>1/2)$ of a population of latent
Bernoulli coins, charging one unit for each observed flip. Algorithms may
create, revisit, and interleave coins and stop under a uniform expected
budget. For any law $\mu$ of $D=|P-1/2|$ on $(0,1/2]$, the minimax RMS
error is comparable to $\omega_\mu(B^{-1/2})$, where
$\omega_\mu(h)=\E_\mu\min\{1,h/D\}$. One algorithm depending only on
the budget attains this rate for every $\mu$. Unbiased estimation with
uniformly finite variance and expected work is possible exactly when
$I_\mu=\E_\mu D^{-1}<\infty$; the optimal uniform variance--work
product lies between $I_\mu^2/16$ and $713I_\mu^2$.

With the additional global rarity constraint $\theta\le\rho$, the
variational quantity $\Psi_{\mu,\rho}$ gives a general lower bound and
characterizes the power-margin regimes below and above exponent two, but
fails at the boundary. For the fixed quadratic law $F(d)=(4d)^2$, put
$L=1+\log(1/\rho)$. The sharp RMS order is
$\min\{\rho,\sqrt{\rho L(1+\log L)/B}\}$,
whereas $\Psi$ gives only $\min\{\rho,\sqrt{\rho L/B}\}$.
For every known magnitude law, rarity bound and positive budget, the
risk is comparable to an entropy--tail selection functional: a selected
part of the law pays its dyadic inverse-square cost entropy, and the
remainder pays a truncated inverse-gap cost. Selecting all mass outside
the smallest-gap quantile $\rho$ is optimal within a factor $1+2\sqrt2$,
which gives a closed formula. For finite laws that formula is also
proved directly, with explicit constants. A robust stopping program has
an exact four-prior dual, a factor-sixteen comparison with minimax risk,
and convergent finite certificates.

The original RMS law is Lean-checked in both directions, with coefficients
$1/64$ and $512$ and one distribution-blind upper algorithm. The critical
minimax law, exact $\Psi$ comparison and counterexample, variational
optimizer, unbiased characterization with coefficients $1/16$ and $713$,
confidence characterization, magnitude-oracle lower bounds, and the
rare-event rate above exponent two are also Lean-checked in their stated
models. The entropy--tail law, the closed quantile form, the
stopping-program results, and the remaining rare-event regimes are paper
proofs. This manuscript consolidates the statements, proofs,
dependencies, and verification boundaries.
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}
We estimate the fraction $\theta=\Prob(P>1/2)$ of a population of latent
Bernoulli coins, charging one unit for each observed flip. Algorithms may
create, revisit, and interleave coins and stop under a uniform expected
budget. For any law $\mu$ of $D=|P-1/2|$ on $(0,1/2]$, the minimax RMS
error is comparable to $\omega_\mu(B^{-1/2})$, where
$\omega_\mu(h)=\E_\mu\min\{1,h/D\}$. One algorithm depending only on
the budget attains this rate for every $\mu$. Unbiased estimation with
uniformly finite variance and expected work is possible exactly when
$I_\mu=\E_\mu D^{-1}<\infty$; the optimal uniform variance--work
product lies between $I_\mu^2/16$ and $713I_\mu^2$.

With the additional global rarity constraint $\theta\le\rho$, the
variational quantity $\Psi_{\mu,\rho}$ gives a general lower bound and
has an explicit optimizer. It characterizes the power-margin regimes
below and above exponent two, but fails at the boundary. For the fixed
quadratic law $F(d)=(4d)^2$, put $L=1+\log(1/\rho)$. The sharp RMS order is
$\min\{\rho,\sqrt{\rho L(1+\log L)/B}\}$,
whereas $\Psi$ gives only $\min\{\rho,\sqrt{\rho L/B}\}$.
The extra factor comes from sign testing across hidden difficulty scales.
The lower argument obtains one hard population pair for all capped
single-coin policies and then transfers it to interacting population
algorithms.

The original RMS law is Lean-checked in both directions, with coefficients
$1/64$ and $512$ and one distribution-blind upper algorithm. The critical
minimax law, exact $\Psi$ comparison and counterexample, untruncated 713
construction, and variational optimizer are also Lean-checked in their
stated models. The critical upper coefficient is $100000$, without a claim
of optimality. The unbiased characterization, its $1/16$ lower coefficient,
and a single law-independent 713 construction are proved in the original
observable model. The confidence characterization is also Lean-checked,
with coefficients $1/64$ and $8192$ and a single upper family depending
only on budget and failure probability. The stronger revealed- and
selected-magnitude lower bounds are also checked, with the paper's RMS
and confidence coefficients. Other rare-event regimes retain
paper-proof status. This manuscript consolidates
the statements, proofs, dependencies, and verification boundaries.
\end{abstract}

## The upper constant: 12665 to 713

The three occurrences of `12665` in the unchanged v4 TeX are:

| Paper location | Original claim | Subsequent result |
|---|---|---|
| [Abstract, line 65](paper.tex#L65) | An unbiased multilevel estimator has variance–work product at most $12665I^2$. | The modified classifier with $(k_1,k_2)=(1/4,1)$ has product strictly below $713I^2$. |
| [§2, Theorem 2, equation `eq:product`, line 283](paper.tex#L283) | The infimum of products of uniform bounds is at most $12665I^2$. | The later construction improves the uniform upper constant; the exact Lean statement is the per-law strict inequality and unbiasedness below. |
| [§6, sufficiency argument, line 776](paper.tex#L776) | The proof uses $85\cdot149=12665$. | The later conditional moment/work bounds use 23 and 31, with product 713. |

Here $I=\mathbb E[D^{-1}]<\infty$. `ThresholdHC.Uniform713.full_bound_713` in [FullBound.lean](../lean/ThresholdHC/Uniform713/FullBound.lean) proves integrability, unbiasedness, and $\operatorname{Var}(Y)\mathbb E W<713I^2$ under [FullBound713Statement](../lean/ThresholdHC/Uniform713/Specification.lean). `ThresholdHC.Uniform713.conditional_bounds` in [Conditional.lean](../lean/ThresholdHC/Uniform713/Conditional.lean) proves the corresponding conditional second moment below $23/d$ and expected work below $31/d$. The constants are uniform in magnitude/sign laws; taking separate suprema over sign functions yields a product at most $713I^2$. Strictness of a pointwise inequality is not automatically strictness after taking suprema.

This is a changed classifier and tighter analysis, not a correction to the original $(1,1)$ estimator. In particular, the original 85 and 149 also appear in §2, Lemma 8 in §4.1, and the calibration and minimax upper proofs in §5. The later argument does not rewrite those sections or replace every numerical constant in their algorithms. The [713 proof document](../proposals/constants/UNIFORM_PRODUCT_BOUND.md) explains the tighter error envelope, one-sided survival bound, and shared-prefix identity $\tau_{N/2}\le\tau_N$, which removes a factor two from the coupled-work bound. Its [independent agent review](../proposals/RESEARCH_REVIEW.md#uniform-product-bound-713) predates the completed formalization; [the kernel audit](../results/uniform713_status.json) records the later complete Lean result. Optimality of 713 is not asserted.

## What remains unchanged in scope

The unrestricted minimax law and the uniform unbiasedness converse retain paper-proof status. Neither the optimizer identity nor the upper-bracket calculations settle a matching arbitrary-law rare-event upper bound. A subsequent [separate proof](../research/2026-10-27-route-a/construction.tex) now establishes the complete power-margin regime $\alpha>2$, with two agent reviews, while $\alpha=2$ remains open. It gives $R_{\mu,\rho}(B)\asymp_\alpha\min\{\rho,\sqrt\rho/(a\sqrt B)\}$ for known rarity and the global constraint on $q$; it is not Lean-checked. [Evidence and limits](../docs/RESULTS.md#rare-event-power-margins-above-two) distinguish this result from the arbitrary-law conjecture. The original expected-work, observation-independence, and confidence-radius limitations still apply; see [v4 §10](paper.tex#L1280) and [the preserved continuation's conjecture](../inputs/next-phase/report/research_note.tex#L246).
